# Plan Library

Standard phases and tasks for an accelerator mastersheet, generalised from the KUEC pre-program plan. Timings are relative to kickoff (K = kickoff Monday; K-10w = ten weeks before). Adapt names, owners and durations to the program, drop what doesn't apply, and add program-specific work. Use the client's short name in approval rows (e.g. "KUEC APPROVAL: …").

Owner labels: use organisation names as the source documents do ("Brinc", "KUEC", "Brinc & KUEC"), not people, unless the user gives named owners.

## Phase 1: Program Architecture & Blueprint (K-10w to K-5w)
- Confirm governance and operating model with the client: steering committee, escalation path, decision rights (K-10w, 1w)
- Build program Gantt with milestones and dependencies (K-10w, 1w)
- Design and finalise the milestone-based curriculum: topics, KPI reviews, investor-prep sequence (K-10w, 3w)
- ✔ APPROVAL: curriculum sign-off (end of curriculum task, 3 days)
- Build startup scoring scorecards and application form (commercial and technical) (K-8w, 2w)
- ✔ APPROVAL: scoring framework and form (2 days after)
- Define KPI framework, milestone gates and assessment templates (baseline, mid-point, final) (K-8w, 2w)
- Write and circulate Program Operating Manual v1.0 (K-7w, 2w)
- ★ Operating Manual v1.0 approved · ★ Curriculum signed off

## Phase 2: Mentor / EIR Sourcing & Onboarding (K-9w to K-1w)
- Build mentor/EIR longlist across target sectors (K-9w, 2w)
- Conduct structured interviews: expertise, coaching style, empathy (K-7w, 3w)
- Score and rank candidates against defined criteria (K-7w, 3w)
- ✔ APPROVAL: shortlist review, sector fit and cultural alignment (K-4w, 1w)
- Confirm shortlist and share with the client (K-4w, 2w)
- Design matching methodology and run intro/chemistry sessions (K-3w, 2w)
- Issue engagement agreements and SLAs (session cadence, response time) (K-3w, 2w)
- ✔ APPROVAL: final mentor roster (K-2w)
- Onboard mentors: handbook, calendar, founder profiles; brief on KPI framework (K-2w, 1w)
- ★ Mentor shortlist confirmed · ★ 100% mentors onboarded before Day 1

## Phase 3: Startup Sourcing & Selection (K-10w to K-1w)
- Activate partner referral channels (universities, VCs, corporates, government) (K-10w, 2w)
- Applications window open, with outreach campaign (K-9w, 4w)
- Initial qualification and screening against gate criteria (K-8w, 4w, overlaps the window)
- Structured scoring with calibrated reviewers (K-5w, 2w)
- Founder interviews (K-4w, 1w)
- Technical and commercial due diligence (K-4w, 2w)
- Compile evaluation report per startup (K-4w, 2w)
- Selection committee review: joint operator and client (K-3w, 1w)
- ✔ APPROVAL: selection committee sign-off (K-3w)
- Confirm final cohort and waitlist (K-3w)
- ✔ APPROVAL: final cohort, written sign-off (K-2w)
- Issue acceptance letters; prepare waitlist for replacements (K-2w, 1w)
- ★ Acceptance letters sent: cohort confirmed

## Phase 4: Program Operations Stack & Systems (K-8w to K-1w)
- Launch CRM/database: pipeline, mentor database, KPI tracking (K-8w, 3w)
- Deploy founder portal: handbook, resources, milestone tracker (K-6w, 3w)
- Organise document layer: templates, reporting archive (K-7w, 3w)
- Configure scheduling for mentor bookings and office hours (K-5w, 2w)
- Configure KPI dashboard and reporting templates (K-5w, 3w)
- Set up cohort comms channels and communication norms (K-2w, 2w)
- Test all systems end to end before Day 1 (K-1w, 1w)
- ★ All systems live

## Phase 5: Founder Onboarding & Launch Prep (K-2w to K)
- Collect company data, cap tables and IP documentation (K-2w, 1w)
- Send welcome email and letter from the program director (K-1w)
- Share portal, drive and comms access (K-1w)
- Run baseline diagnostics and set Milestone 1 targets per startup (K-1w, 1w)
- ✔ APPROVAL: pre-launch checklist and go/no-go (K-1w)
- ★ Founders onboarded: KPIs baselined

## Delivery phases (program-specific, after K)
Mirror the curriculum's phases, one block each, with:
- Workshops and sessions for the phase; mentor 1:1s at the agreed cadence
- Checkpoint KPI review at the end of the phase
- ★ Phase complete
Include where relevant:
- Mid-point review per startup (about halfway) and ✔ APPROVAL of any re-matching or scope changes
- Corporate and investor introductions sprint
- Demo Day preparation: venue, investor invitations, pitch coaching, rehearsals (last 6 weeks)
- ✔ APPROVAL: Demo Day plan and budget
- ★ Demo Day

## Close-out (after the last session)
- Final KPI assessment per startup
- Impact report and board summary
- Close-out review with the client: lessons learned, next-edition recommendations
- ✔ APPROVAL: program completion sign-off
- ★ Program closed

## Ongoing: Governance & Reporting (spans the whole plan)
- Weekly program team standup
- Client update at the agreed cadence (weekly or bi-weekly)
- Monthly steering committee meeting
- End-of-week email update to stakeholders
- Monthly reporting and executive summaries
