# Rubric Library

Starting points only. Adapt criteria and weights to the program, confirm with the user, and calibrate anchors with reviewers before final decisions.

## A. Startup accelerator / scale program (1–5)

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Market traction & commercial validation | 30% |
| Technology & differentiation | 25% |
| Scalability & growth potential | 20% |
| Strategic fit with sponsor/ecosystem | 15% |
| Team & execution | 10% |

Anchors — Market traction:
1 no evidence of demand · 2 anecdotal interest · 3 pilots or LOIs · 4 active users and/or early revenue · 5 multiple paying customers or scaled pilots.

Technology: 1 no clear differentiation · 2 claimed, not shown · 3 demonstrated advantage · 4 clear IP and technical depth · 5 defensible, validated moat.

Scalability: 1 no repeatable model · 2 bespoke/unclear · 3 repeatable in one market · 4 early expansion evidence · 5 proven expansion readiness.

Strategic fit: 1 no link · 2 loose thematic fit · 3 aligned with sponsor priorities · 4 formal affiliation · 5 commercializing sponsor-owned IP or core strategic priority.

Team: 1 essential gaps · 2 domain expertise, little execution · 3 expertise + visible execution · 4 complementary team with delivery record · 5 prior scale experience.

Typical gates: stage, sector/eligibility, affiliation or IP requirement, legal entity/location.

## B. Pre-accelerator / incubator (1–5)

Problem clarity 25% · Customer insight & validation 25% · Solution feasibility 20% · Team commitment & coachability 20% · Fit with program 10%.

## C. Hackathon / buildathon judging (1–5 or 1–10)

Problem relevance to challenge 20% · Innovation/originality 20% · Technical execution & working demo 25% · User/business impact 20% · Pitch & clarity 15%.
For AI buildathons, consider splitting technical execution into "working prototype" and "responsible/robust AI use".

## D. Innovation challenge (corporate / government)

Strategic alignment with challenge statement 25% · Solution maturity (TRL) 20% · Pilot feasibility within program timeframe 20% · Impact & scalability 20% · Team & partner readiness 15%.

## E. Mentor / EIR / judge selection (1–5)

Domain or sector expertise 30% · Coaching/mentoring style & empathy 25% · Track record with founders/teams 20% · Communication quality 15% · Availability vs program commitment 10%.

## F. Founder interview (1–5, unweighted unless specified)

Clarity of vision · Ambition · Coachability · Commercial openness · Execution track record.

## G. Due diligence focus areas (1–5)

Product & technical architecture · Technical roadmap · Unit economics & business model · Traction quality & reference checks · Expansion & scalability · (optional) Legal/IP & cap table.
