# Sourcing Playbook

Starting points. Replace with what has worked for the user's past programs.

## Target profile template

- Program: [name] · Applications close: [date] · Cohort size: [n]
- Stage: [e.g. post-revenue, seed to Series A] · Sector: [focus or open]
- Geography / residency: [e.g. UAE-based or willing to relocate]
- Must have (gates): [e.g. university IP, legal entity, product in market]
- Strong signals: [pilots, paying customers, technical founders with commercial openness]
- Not a fit: [e.g. idea stage, service agencies, no product]

## Channels

| Channel | Good for | Typical owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| University / TTO / lab referrals | Research-backed and IP-based startups | Client or partner liaison | Give partners a forwardable blurb and a referral code or field |
| VC and angel networks | Funded, growth-stage teams | Operator partnerships lead | Ask for 3–5 named intros, not a blast |
| Corporate innovation teams | Startups with pilot potential | Client | Works best when there's a pilot challenge statement |
| Government and free-zone programs | Registered local startups | Client | Check eligibility overlaps with other programs |
| Alumni and past applicants | Warm leads, waitlist | Operator | Re-invite strong waitlisted teams first |
| Communities and events | Early and technical founders | Program team | Short talk + QR to the form; collect consent for follow-up |
| Open call (web, LinkedIn, media) | Volume and reach | Comms | Track with a source field or UTM link |
| Direct outreach | Specific high-fit targets | Program team | Highest effort; keep it personal and short |

## Funnel tracking fields (add to the application form or base)

Source channel · Referring partner / person · Campaign or UTM · Applied date · Gate result · Screening score · Stage reached.

## Outreach checklist

- One reason this program fits them, in their terms
- The deadline and time commitment
- What they get (one or two concrete things)
- One link, one ask
- A named sender and a reply path
- Under 120 words for email, under 60 for chat

## Pace check

Applications needed by deadline ÷ days left = daily pace needed. Compare with the last 7 days' actual pace. If actual is under 70% of needed, push the highest-quality channel first, then extend reach.
