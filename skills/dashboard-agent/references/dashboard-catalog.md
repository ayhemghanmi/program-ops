# Dashboard Catalog

Panels by area. Build the ones the program has data for; show the rest as empty states naming the missing source.

## Overview
- Phase and week of program; days to next milestone and to demo day/finals
- Headline KPIs vs target with direction
- Next 5 milestones and pending client approvals (gate, owner, due)
- Top 3 risks with owner
- Data health summary

## Plan and approvals (mastersheet / plan)
- Tasks by phase and status; overdue and due in 14 days
- Approval gates: pending, signed, late
- Milestones hit vs planned
- Staleness flag when most items are past due but unchanged

## Sourcing
- Applications vs target over time, days to deadline, daily pace needed vs actual
- Applications by channel and by referring partner (with field fill rate)
- Quality by source: gate pass rate and average screening score
- Target-list outreach: contacted → replied → applied

## Selection
- Funnel: applied → gated → screened → shortlisted → interviewed → DD → accepted / waitlist / reject
- Ranked shortlist with composite and per-criterion scores, weights shown
- Reviewer calibration: gaps of 2+ points, reviewer means
- Committee decisions and rationale status

## Cohort and participants
- Roster: sector, stage, location, mentor, status
- Per participant page: profile, scores, KPI baseline → latest → target, mentor and session log, open actions, deliverables, latest notes (audience-filtered)

## KPIs
- Program KPIs vs targets
- Participant KPIs: % to target, movement since baseline, checkpoint values (e.g. Month 0/4/8)
- Revenue, pilots, partnerships, funding raised (only where reported, with source and date)

## Mentors / EIRs
- Pool: status, sectors, focus areas, capacity used vs available
- Matches: participant ↔ mentor, rationale, start date
- Sessions delivered vs commitment per month; days since last session
- Satisfaction pulse trend per pair; alerts (two drops, score ≤ 2, unreviewed past SLA)

## Workshops and sessions
- Calendar: upcoming sessions with lead and attendees
- Attendance by session and by participant
- Feedback: overall rating, NPS, top takeaways, requests for 1:1 support

## Meetings and actions
- Recent meetings with link to recording or minutes
- Open actions by owner and due date; overdue actions
- Decisions log

## Partners and leads
- Investor and corporate interest by participant; follow-up status
- Pilots in discussion / signed

## Risks and issues
- Risk register: category, impact, owner, status, last update

## Data health (always on)
- Source per panel and last refresh time
- Records excluded as test or dummy
- Empty or low-fill fields that block a panel
- Conflicts between sources (e.g. two figures for the same metric)
