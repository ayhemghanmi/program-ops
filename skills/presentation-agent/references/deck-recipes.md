# Deck Recipes

Starting structures. Merge or drop slides to fit the content.

## Kickoff (14–18 slides)
Cover · Welcome / why this program · Objectives · Who's in the room (partners, team) · Program at a glance (duration, cohort, format) · Phases timeline · Founder/participant journey · Mentors/EIR model · Operating rhythm (weekly → monthly) · Tools & where things live · Expectations & commitments (both sides) · KPIs & checkpoints · Key milestones & dates · First 30 days · Contacts · Close.

## Monthly / steering committee report (6–10)
Headline status · Progress vs plan (milestones, approvals) · KPI dashboard · Participant highlights · Mentor/session delivery & satisfaction · Risks & mitigations · Decisions needed · Next month.

## Selection committee pack (per cohort)
Process recap & funnel · Rubric & weights · Ranked shortlist table · One slide per finalist (snapshot, scores, strengths, concerns, DD flags, recommendation) · Borderline cases for discussion · Suggested cohort + waitlist · Decisions to record.

## Workshop / session (8–15)
Title & facilitator · Objective / what you'll leave with · Agenda & timing · Core frameworks (1 idea per slide) · Example/case · Exercise instructions (time-boxed) · Debrief questions · Actions & next session.

## Demo day (event + per-team)
Opening · Program story & impact numbers · Cohort overview · Team order · Per-team title cards · Partners & thanks · Close/networking.

## Hackathon / buildathon (briefing + finals)
Briefing: challenge statements · rules & timeline · judging criteria · resources & mentors · submission requirements. Finals: finalists · judging recap · winners by category · next steps for teams.

## Proposal (10–14)
Context & client goals · Our understanding · Approach · Program design · Timeline · Team · Track record · KPIs & reporting · Commercials (if requested) · Next steps.

## Close-out / impact (10–14)
Headline outcomes · Funnel & participation · KPI results vs targets · Participant stories · Partnerships & investment · Feedback scores · Lessons learned · Recommendations for next edition · Thanks.
